# Legal bounds

StyxNet is designed for lawful open-source intelligence, defensive research, threat analysis, and authorised investigations.

## Is StyxNet illegal?

No. StyxNet is not an illegal tool. It is a passive OSINT platform that reads public pages and organises public evidence.

However, legality depends on:

- Your jurisdiction
- The target and the method used
- Platform terms of service
- Data-protection and privacy law
- Whether you have authorisation for the investigation

## What StyxNet does not do

- It does not provide unauthorised access
- It does not supply stolen credentials
- It does not provide exploitation or bypass instructions
- It does not authorise harassment, doxxing, or stalking

## Operator responsibility

Operators are responsible for complying with applicable law, platform terms, and ethical standards. Do not use StyxNet to target private individuals, interfere with systems, or handle data unlawfully.
