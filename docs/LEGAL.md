# Legal use

StyxNet is a **legal, passive OSINT platform**. It is designed to collect and organise publicly available information for lawful research, defensive intelligence, platform safety, and threat analysis.

## OSINT and public sources

OSINT is the practice of gathering information from publicly available sources. Public-source OSINT is generally lawful. StyxNet is built around that principle: it reads public pages and organises public evidence.

StyxNet does **not** access private systems, bypass authentication, obtain stolen credentials, or provide exploitation or intrusion capabilities.

## Why StyxNet is legal

- It reads public pages only
- It does not provide unauthorised access
- It does not supply stolen credentials
- It does not provide exploitation or bypass instructions
- It is designed for defensive, research, and platform-safety use
- It is operated within legal and ethical bounds

## Do I need special authorisation?

Public-source OSINT generally does **not** require special authorisation. You do not need to be a government agency or licensed investigator to read public information.

That said, specific laws, contracts, platform terms, privacy rules, or the context of an investigation may impose additional requirements. Those requirements come from the law and the platform, not from StyxNet.

## Operator responsibilities

Operators are responsible for:

- Complying with applicable law
- Respecting platform terms and rate limits
- Respecting privacy and data-protection rules
- Avoiding harassment, doxxing, stalking, or targeting private individuals
- Not using results to interfere with systems or people
- Treating a confirmed handle or content match as evidence, not absolute identity or ownership

## What StyxNet does not authorise

StyxNet does not authorise:

- Unauthorised access to systems or accounts
- Credential theft or misuse
- Harassment, doxxing, or stalking
- Interference with platforms or individuals
- Unlawful handling of personal data

## Legal note

This document is not legal advice. For high-risk, regulated, or jurisdiction-specific matters, consult qualified counsel. That does not change the core position: StyxNet is a legal passive OSINT platform, and public-source research is generally lawful.
