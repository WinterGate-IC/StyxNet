# Overview

StyxNet is a passive multi-surface OSINT intelligence platform built by the WinterGate Intelligence Collective.

It is designed to collect public evidence, correlate identities and targets, validate findings, and preserve an auditable record. It is not a single-purpose scraper. It is an orchestration layer for collection and validation.

## Positioning

Traditional OSINT workflows are fragmented: one tool for search, one for usernames, one for onion indexing, one for notes, one for validation. StyxNet combines those functions into one system with shared targets, observations, evidence, and validation state.

## Core principles

- **Public-source only** — no unauthorised access.
- **Evidence first** — findings are linked to page-level signals.
- **Confirmed-only active intel** — weak or irrelevant records are quarantined.
- **Auditability** — actions and retention are logged.
- **Defensive purpose** — research, platform safety, and threat analysis.

## What StyxNet is not

- Not a hacking tool
- Not a credential or exploitation service
- Not a target list
- Not a doxxing or harassment platform
- Not a guarantee of identity or ownership
