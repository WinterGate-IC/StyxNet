# FAQ

## Is StyxNet an illegal tool?

No. It is a passive OSINT platform for lawful use. Legality still depends on jurisdiction, target, method, and platform terms.

## Does StyxNet access private data?

No. It is designed around public sources and does not provide unauthorised access.

## What does “Confirmed” mean?

It means page-level evidence links the handle or content to the finding. It does not mean legal ownership or absolute identity.

## Why are records quarantined?

Unverified, junk, legacy, or irrelevant records are quarantined so they do not pollute active intelligence. They are deleted after three days.

## Is StyxNet omniscient?

No. Coverage depends on source availability, Tor/proxy health, platform blocking, and gated content.

## Is the source code in this repository?

No. This repository is public documentation and scoring only.
