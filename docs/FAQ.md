# FAQ

## Is StyxNet an illegal tool?

No. StyxNet is a legal, passive OSINT platform built for lawful public-source research.

## Is OSINT legal?

Yes. Public-source OSINT is generally lawful. It becomes unlawful only when the method crosses into unauthorised access, privacy violations, harassment, or other prohibited conduct.

## Do I need special authorisation to use StyxNet?

No special authorisation is generally required for public-source research. You do not need to be a government agency or licensed investigator to read public information. Specific laws, contracts, platform terms, or investigation context may add requirements, but those come from the law and the platform, not from StyxNet.

## Does StyxNet access private data?

No. It is designed around public sources and does not access private systems or bypass authentication.

## What does “Confirmed” mean?

It means page-level evidence links the handle or content to the finding. It does not mean legal ownership or absolute identity.

## Why are records quarantined?

Unverified, junk, legacy, or irrelevant records are quarantined so they do not pollute active intelligence. They are deleted after three days.

## Is StyxNet omniscient?

No. Coverage depends on source availability, Tor/proxy health, platform blocking, and gated content.
