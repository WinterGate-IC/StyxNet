# Responsible use

StyxNet is for lawful, defensive, and research use.

## Do

- Use public sources lawfully
- Respect platform terms and rate limits
- Preserve evidence and provenance
- Minimise collection of personal data
- Report serious harm through proper channels
- Follow disclosure and privacy law

## Do not

- Access private or restricted systems
- Harass, stalk, or dox individuals
- Publish private identifiers
- Use results to incite harm
- Treat a handle match as absolute identity

## Disclosure

If you believe StyxNet or its documentation creates a security or safety issue, follow [`SECURITY.md`](../SECURITY.md).
